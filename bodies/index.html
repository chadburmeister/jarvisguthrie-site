The Book

Step Out of the Boat

At eighteen, one drunken decision cost Jarvis Guthrie his future — an Army contract, a college scholarship, his freedom. This book is what he found on the other side of it: a calling he never asked for, and a faith that has since sent him back into prisons, onto death row, and into the lives of men everyone else had given up on.

“To be able to encourage that young Jarvis to step out of the boat, put all your faith and trust in God. And He's going to use you to change the lives of many, many people.”

The Road Here

It isn't a theory he read about.

It's the road he walked — turned into a story other people can walk through. This is the short version. The book is the rest of it.

“Going to prison as an inmate was my greatest experience — it birthed my purpose. We are not labels of crime, but products of second chances.”

JARVIS GUTHRIE
JANUARY 21, 2012

One night, one decision

Eighteen years old, enlisted in the Army while taking college courses — and a drunken walk to the wrong house that ended with a gun in his face and handcuffs on a hospital bed by morning.

2012–2014

Incarcerated — and found

Two years in Florida State prison, three years on house arrest. Eight months in, he met the pastor — a retired police officer — who would become his spiritual father.

MAY 13, 2013

The calling

After a three-day fast, Jarvis received his call to become “an ambassador of the gospel” — the turning point Ephesians 6:19–20 still names for him today.

APRIL 2, 2014

Released — and turned back

He walked out on a Wednesday and went straight to church. Four days later, he was enrolled in college.

2021 → TODAY

Back inside, on purpose

Prison ministry at Lawtey Correctional Institution every Friday night, and death row ministry at Florida State Prison — the same seed his pastor planted in him, planted again in the next man.

TODAY

Grace House — and the book

Jarvis and his wife, Jennifer, are building Saved By Grace House together. And now, a book written to reach the men he can't visit in person.

What Jarvis Does

Five ways he lives out the calling.

Preaching & Teaching

Ordained in September 2024 — bringing the same testimony to pulpits and congregations across Jacksonville.

Prison & Reentry Ministry

Weekly ministry at Lawtey Correctional Institution, and death row ministry at Florida State Prison.

Fatherhood Advocacy

Championing fathers and supporting families impacted by the child welfare and criminal justice systems.

Mentoring Men

One-on-one walks with men rebuilding after incarceration, addiction, or a season that took everything.

Author, Step Out of the Boat

Writing the book so the message can reach further than any one visit ever could — into prison libraries nationwide.

Founder & Leader — Saved By Grace House

A Christ-centered shared living home in Jacksonville, built with his wife Jennifer, where the whole philosophy above becomes a household.
